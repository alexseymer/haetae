# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This is a short summary; the full text there applies where this page is silent.

## Our pledge

We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful; give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow them, and may temporarily or permanently ban a person for behavior they deem inappropriate.

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately using GitHub's private reporting: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, and say in the title that it is a Code of Conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) in proportion to the behavior.
